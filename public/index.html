About

This is a web game based on the anime series Kakegurui, from episode 3 "The Untouchable Girl".

How the game works

There are 4 types of cards: 0, 1, 2 and 3


Each player gets 4 cards at hand.


In each turn, each player plays a single card from their hand.


Each turn adds the card's value to the total.
If the card you play brings the total above 9, you lose.


Controls

Mouse: hover a card and click to play.
Keyboard: 1–4 play directly · ←→ navigate · Enter throw · R reshuffle.
Controller: D-pad / left stick to navigate · A to throw · Y to reshuffle.
